## 1. Design & Motion Choreography

- **Cover**: Dark backdrop fades in while 5 red LED indicators illuminate sequentially at 40ms intervals.
- **Load**: All 5 lights hold at full glow while the next route loads.
- **Reveal**: Lights out instantly, backdrop fades out (0.35s ease-out).
